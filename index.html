How to use it

  1. Pick a lesson or press “Next”. The key you need lights up on the keyboard.
  2. Type the highlighted letter. Correct letters turn bright; a wrong key flashes red.
  3. You do not need to change your computer language — the app maps your physical keys to Arabic letters.
  4. Every mistake is recorded. Open the Mistake Focus tab to generate a drill built from your own weak letters.
  5. Follow your progress in the My Progress tab with the per-letter accuracy heatmap.
  6. Switch between Lesson, Time and Words at the top. In Time mode the clock starts on your first keystroke.
  7. Press Tab while typing to instantly restart the current test. Change the Arabic font from the Font menu.

Tip: keep your fingers on the home row and use the correct finger for each key.
